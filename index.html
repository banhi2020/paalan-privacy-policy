Paalan Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

Paalan is a baby health tracking app used by parents to record a child's vaccinations, growth measurements, developmental milestones, and feeding. Paalan is a local-only app: everything you enter stays on your own device and is never uploaded to a server we control.

Information the app collects

All of the following is entered manually by you and stored only on your device: your child's name, date of birth, sex, birth weight/length, blood group, and gestational age; vaccination records (vaccine name, dates, clinic name, batch numbers); growth measurements; developmental milestone check-ins; feeding and meal logs, food preferences, and any noted reactions; medicine logs; and household details you choose to add (diet type, region).

Paalan does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or location, and does not request any permissions beyond what's needed to run the app itself.

How this information is used

Your data powers the features you see in the app: generating the vaccination schedule, plotting WHO growth percentiles, tracking milestones, giving nutrition guidance, and preparing the Doctor Summary PDF you can choose to export. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond showing it back to you inside the app.

Data storage and security

Paalan stores all data locally on your device in an embedded database. There is no backend server, and nothing you enter is transmitted off your device during normal use. If you export a Doctor Summary PDF, that file is created on your device and only leaves it when you actively choose to share it through your device's native share options — at that point, the recipient controls that copy.

If you uninstall the app or erase its data, this information is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered by us, since we never held a copy of it.

Third-party services

Paalan uses a crash and error reporting service to help us find and fix bugs. It only ever receives technical diagnostic information — device type, OS version, app version, and error details — never your child's health data.

Children's data

Paalan is designed for use by parents and caregivers about their children — it is not intended for children to use directly, and the app does not knowingly collect information directly from children. Because the information in the app concerns a minor, we treat it with particular care: it never leaves your device on its own, is never sold, and is never used for advertising. If you are a parent or guardian with questions about your child's data in this app, contact us using the details below.

Your rights and control over your data

Since all data lives only on your device, you're always in full control of it:

  • View or edit any entry at any time within the app
  • Delete a specific record (a growth measurement, a vaccine entry, and so on) from its screen
  • Permanently delete everything using "Delete all data" in Settings, or by uninstalling the app
  • Export a Doctor Summary PDF to share with your pediatrician, entirely by your own choice

We don't hold a copy of your data to delete on our end, because we never received one.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app's features change. Material changes will be reflected in an updated version of this page, with a new effective date at the top.
